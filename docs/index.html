What LinguaFast does

More ways to be understood.

Practical tools for everyday travel, friendships, work, and conversations across languages.

🌐

Text translation

Translate typed messages with automatic source-language detection when available, or choose a language yourself.

🎙

Voice and playback

Speak to enter text and listen to translations read aloud using the services installed on your Android device.

👥

Three-speaker conversations

Give Speaker A, Speaker B, and Guest C their own language settings, voice controls, and conversation history.

✨

Optional AI Polish

Ask your selected AI provider to suggest spelling and grammar improvements before you choose whether to apply them.

🔐

Encrypted provider keys

Keep separate API credentials for each configured AI provider in local Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage.

📚

History and saved phrases

Find previous translations, retain useful phrases, manage local history and make manual backups without exporting AI keys.

Conversation mode

One conversation.
Three voices.

LinguaFast's conversation view isn't limited to two people. Each speaker can use a chosen language, with a dedicated microphone and a shared transcript.

Automatic recognition and transcription depend on the Android speech service. If a short phrase is misunderstood, select the language manually or type it instead.

Your AI, your choice

Switch providers. Keep your keys.

Version 1.23 introduced independent saved profiles for every supported AI provider. Switch back without typing your previous API key again; models and endpoint settings are remembered too.

Data and trust

Your words are yours to manage.

LinguaFast stores history and settings locally where available, and provides retention, deletion and backup controls. AI keys are not included in exported conversation backups.

Read the privacy policy
Know where text goes

Translation needs providers.

Instant translations go to online translation services. Optional AI requests send text to your selected AI provider. Android's speech provider may process audio remotely. Read the privacy information before sending sensitive material.
