What LinguaFast does

More ways to be understood.

Practical tools for everyday travel, friendships, work, and conversations across languages.

🌐

Text translation

Translate typed messages with automatic source-language detection when available, or choose a language yourself.

🎙

Voice and playback

Speak using Android’s installed speech recognizer, then listen to translated text. Speech recognition is separate from AI model selection and depends on the language and device service.

👥

Three-speaker conversations

Give Speaker A, Speaker B, and Guest C their own language settings, voice controls, and conversation history.

✨

Optional AI Polish

One tap corrects the active text draft using your chosen AI provider; Undo restores the exact previous wording locally, without another AI request. Available in Translate and typed Conversation drafts.

🔐

Encrypted provider keys

Keep separate API credentials for each configured AI provider in local Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage.

📚

Saved phrases for learning

Open full original and translated text together in History and Saved, copy both languages, or play each aloud. A dedicated learning system is still in development.

Recent app improvements

Clearer translation. Clearer updates.

What changed in the latest published Android builds.

📥

Updates you can follow

The v34 updater streams the GitHub APK over HTTPS on modern Android to avoid downloads stuck on Waiting. It reports bytes received, supports cancellation and verifies the published SHA-256 twice before Android asks for installation approval. This new download method still needs an end-to-end device test.

🗣

Cleaner v34 layout

Original and translated text appear in aligned, clearly outlined boxes. AI provider settings and the reusable phrases, History and Saved library expand when needed, reducing vertical scrolling without removing features.

⚙️

Provider selection

Choose Instant or AI, open the compact AI channel selector to change providers, or manage saved profiles in Settings. Remembered keys use Android Keystore-backed encryption.

Speech recognition is provided by Android, not Gemini or the selected AI translation model. See speech-recognition troubleshooting if transcription is unclear.

Learn from everyday conversations

Save it. Review it. Remember it.

LinguaFast is expanding from a translation tool into a practical language-learning companion. Save phrases you find useful, revisit the original text and translation, and deliberately choose when to reuse a phrase in the translator.

Your study library stays separate from live conversation history. Opening a saved phrase for review should not change who is speaking or send a message to anyone.

Translation accuracy can vary. Saved translations are study references, not verified lessons; important wording should be checked with a fluent speaker. The separate learning interface is in development and may not yet be available in the published APK.

Conversation mode

One conversation.
Three voices.

LinguaFast's conversation view isn't limited to two people. Each speaker can use a chosen language, with a dedicated microphone and a shared transcript.

Android’s speech-recognition service converts microphone audio into text before the translation provider receives it. If transcription is garbled, choose the speaker’s actual language instead of Auto, speak away from background noise, and review the recognized text. Changing Gemini or another translation model does not change the Android recognizer.

Learn how it works
Your AI, your choice

Switch providers. Keep your keys.

The Android app has an expandable main-screen AI-provider selector and distinct editable provider profiles. Keys, models and endpoint settings are remembered separately when you choose local storage. The Settings dialog stays open after saving so you can configure another provider.

Data and trust

Your words are yours to manage.

LinguaFast stores history and settings locally where available, and provides retention, deletion and backup controls. AI keys are not included in exported conversation backups.

Read the privacy policy
Know where text goes

Translation needs providers.

Instant translations go to online translation services. Optional AI requests send text to your selected AI provider. Android's speech provider may process audio remotely. Read the privacy information before sending sensitive material.
