Privacy policy

What LinguaFast uses, what stays on the device, and where requests go.

Product: LinguaFast for Android, application ID com.linguafast.translatox
Developer credit: Stylish Future Technologies
Last reviewed: 21 November 2026
Release basis: publicly published LinguaFast v1.34.0 (build 34)

LinguaFast is a translation application. The current application uses online translation services and optional third-party AI providers. Please avoid sending confidential material that you do not want a provider to process. We do not claim that third-party services erase or keep your requests for any particular length of time.

1. Information used

LinguaFast processes typed messages, chosen language settings, voice-to-text transcripts, translation results, optional AI Polish requests, application preferences and any conversation records you choose to keep. If you enable speech input, the app requests Android microphone permission when you activate that feature.

The app's own speech flow uses the recognition service installed on your device. Depending on the Android service and its configuration, that provider may process spoken audio locally or remotely; LinguaFast does not guarantee offline speech recognition.

2. Information sent to other services

  • Instant translation: source text and chosen languages are sent over the internet to a translation service. The current app uses a Google translation endpoint with a MyMemory fallback.
  • Optional AI: enabling AI translation or AI Polish sends selected message text and the selected API key as authentication to your configured provider, such as Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek or a custom provider. Long messages may be split into multiple requests. Providers have their own billing, policy, retention and data-use practices.
  • Speech recognition: the installed Android speech service may process audio and return text to LinguaFast. Recognized text is then used for translation as requested.
  • Updates: LinguaFast retrieves public release metadata and, with user approval, an APK from GitHub. In v34 on Android 10+, it streams the APK over HTTPS into a pending Downloads/LinguaFast file and checks the published SHA-256 twice before making it visible and requesting installation; older Android versions use Download Manager. Download cancellation or failed verification removes the pending file. GitHub and network operators may process connection metadata including IP addresses.
  • Manual sharing: if you copy, share or export your records, the selected clipboard or receiving app handles that data under its own controls.

AI Polish runs only when you explicitly tap Polish, and its provider request may use billed tokens. A successful result replaces the active typed or dictated draft; Undo restores the exact previous text locally without a second AI call. An unsuccessful AI request leaves the draft unchanged. Conversation speech messages are not automatically polished. AI providers may decline requests under their policies.

3. Information stored locally

Where local storage is available, LinguaFast keeps history, conversations, saved translations, reusable phrases and preferences on your device. The current limits reflected in the Android app are up to 200 translation-history entries, 200 conversation messages, 200 saved translations and 40 reusable phrases.

AI credentials: in the native Android app (v23 onwards), opted-in remembered API keys are stored per provider in app-private encrypted SharedPreferences protected by an Android Keystore AES-GCM key. When upgrading from v22, the app attempts to migrate the previously saved key only after encryption succeeds. If encryption fails, legacy data may be retained to avoid losing that key. The separate browser edition, where used, relies on optional browser localStorage and does not provide Android Keystore protection.

Selected AI keys are sent to their relevant providers to authorise requests. Keys are not intentionally included in a conversation-history export or stored on the public GitHub release repository. Your device does not sync stored keys across phones.

4. Retention, deletion and backups

The default history and conversation retention period is 365 days; you can select 30 days, 90 days, 365 days or retention until deletion, subject to the record limits. Saved phrases and favourites remain until you delete them or the app's storage rules apply.

Use Privacy and data → Clear local data to remove app records and saved provider credentials. Individual messages and records can also be deleted using their controls. Deleting the app or clearing Android app storage may permanently erase local history because the app does not provide automatic cloud recovery.

Manual JSON backups and copied translation history are unencrypted once placed on your clipboard or in another app. The app's backup excludes AI API keys. You are responsible for storing exported files safely.

Deleting local data does not remove copies already processed or stored by Google, MyMemory, an AI provider, a speech provider, or another destination you chose. Contact those providers about their retention and deletion processes.

5. Android permissions

  • Internet: required for online translation, optional AI requests and update checks.
  • Microphone: used only for speech input when you choose to activate it, subject to Android permission controls.
  • Request install packages: used for the separate GitHub sideload update channel. Android requires your approval before an APK is installed. The Play-distributed build may use a different update mechanism.

6. This website

This public informational site is hosted with GitHub Pages and does not provide sign-in, API key entry, or analytics scripts. Its homepage may query GitHub's public release API to show the latest app version. GitHub may log standard access and security information such as IP addresses under GitHub's own policies. When you follow an external link, the destination site operates independently.

7. Privacy contact and corrections

Developer attribution: Stylish Future Technologies. The product-specific support and contact page currently provides a public GitHub issue route for non-sensitive questions. A verified private contact email and legal controller details are pending before Play Store submission. Do not put sensitive personal information, API keys or private transcripts into a public issue.

Public provider and security information is also available in the source privacy notice and security notice. This policy will be updated when product data handling or support details change; publishing it does not itself establish independent legal compliance or Play Protect clearance.
